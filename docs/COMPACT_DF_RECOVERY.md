# Compact DF recovery: matched-family tests

The first experiment asks whether the regularized exponential stellar DF can
recover an unknown potential from noiseless observations generated by the same
family. It follows the numerical implementation checks in
[REGULARIZED_DF_IMPLEMENTATION.md](REGULARIZED_DF_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

Two equilibria contain 3 million solar masses of stars and a 300,000-solar-mass
Plummer remnant component with scale radius 3 pc. One has no DM. The other has
a cored halo with density 2 solar masses per cubic parsec at 20 pc, scale radius
30 pc, and Gaussian taper radius 1,000 pc. Distance is fixed at 5.43 kpc.
The stellar inputs are J0=300 pc km/s, alpha=1.5, b_out=0.5, and Ja=300 pc km/s.
The anisotropy coordinate b_out is not the velocity anisotropy beta.

Truth models use twice the fitting resolution in potential, velocity, moment,
projection, action, and contour grids. A cold model at the fitting resolution
must reproduce every mock bin within 0.1 adopted errors before fitting starts.
This comparison includes the photometric profile after profiling its zero point.

The mocks retain the saved 89 kinematic bins, stellar radial selection nodes,
proper-motion units, asymmetric errors, and 82 photometric radii. Their values
are replaced by noiseless equilibrium predictions. Streaming is zero, and
every profile samples the same stellar mass/light DF. Photometric errors retain
the earlier diagnostic weighting; neither these errors nor the mock objective
define an adopted inference prior or a calibrated model-selection likelihood.

## Six fits

Both mocks are fitted with a free cored halo from two distinct starting points.
The no-DM mock also has two fits with the halo fixed to zero. Free-halo fits
have nine coordinates: five stellar controls, remnant mass and radius, and halo
density and radius. No-halo fits have seven. Halo density is bounded linearly
between 0 and 10 solar masses per cubic parsec, so zero is permitted exactly.
The halo radius is unidentified at zero density and is not a recovery criterion.
The distance, halo inner slope, and outer taper remain fixed.

The two starting points bracket the injected stellar mass and differ in every
free coordinate. They are the same for the two mocks. The fitter never starts
at the injected parameter vector. Bounded least squares uses coordinates
scaled to [0,1], with a finite-difference step of 0.002 in these coordinates.
The step remains finite at zero halo density. Each attempt is limited to 180
actual equilibrium evaluations, including derivative probes and rejected
trials. A budget-limited fit is recorded as unconverged.

Nearby evaluations can reuse the last converged stellar potential as an
iteration seed. All scientific controls are rebuilt for each trial, and each
selected solution receives both a cold replay and a fully refined cold solve.
Identical coordinate evaluations within an attempt reuse their saved residuals.

## Acceptance criteria

Each fit records these checks separately:

- The optimizer terminates within its budget.
- Cold replay and resolution refinement each shift residuals by less than
  0.1 adopted errors; independent projected moments agree within 0.5%.
- Refined mock residuals have RMS below 0.1 and maximum below 0.3 adopted errors.
- Stellar mass is recovered within 5%, and total enclosed mass within 10%
  at every diagnostic radius from 0.5 to 80 pc.
- Injected nonzero density at 20 pc is recovered within 10%. For the no-DM
  mock, recovered density must be below 0.1 solar masses per cubic parsec.

These are predefined diagnostic thresholds. Agreement of different starts
must also be inspected; no single best run certifies uniqueness. The saved
profiles separate stars, remnants, halo, and total mass. A good total mass
profile need not imply that all components or their scale radii are identified.

## Preservation and execution

The driver freezes source, configurations, tests, and input snapshots. Hashes
are checked before a batch starts or resumes. Model improvements and statuses
are written atomically, and every evaluated trial is appended to a flushed
log. Controller and worker locks prevent duplicate runs. After an interruption,
rerunning the batch restarts unfinished fits from their saved best parameters;
it does not restore optimizer memory. Completed fits, including capped attempts,
are retained and skipped. Extending a capped fit requires an explicitly recorded
follow-up experiment.

```sh
PY=/Users/vasilybelokurov/Work/venvs/.venv/bin/python
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py prepare --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py run --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME --detach
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py report --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME
```

Two single-threaded workers run at most. PNG reports go to `plots/`, with their
arrays and provenance under `results/plot_data/`. The result directory contains
the immutable mocks, per-fit histories, checkpoints, validation, and summaries.

This stage checks recovery and optimization within a matched stellar and dark
density family. Independent lowered-isothermal mocks and fits, halo mismatch,
noise, selection differences, mass-to-light gradients, and real-data inference
follow separately. Passing this stage alone does not establish unbiased DM
inference for Omega Cen.
