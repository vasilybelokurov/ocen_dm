    Compact DF recovery: matched-family tests¶

    The first experiment asks whether the regularized exponential stellar DF can recover an unknown potential from noiseless observations generated by the same family. It follows the numerical implementation checks in REGULARIZED_DF_IMPLEMENTATION.md.

    Two equilibria contain 3 million solar masses of stars and a 300,000-solar-mass Plummer remnant component with scale radius 3 pc. One has no DM. The other has a cored halo with density 2 solar masses per cubic parsec at 20 pc, scale radius 30 pc, and Gaussian taper radius 1,000 pc. Distance is fixed at 5.43 kpc. The stellar inputs are J0=300 pc km/s, alpha=1.5, b_out=0.5, and Ja=300 pc km/s. The anisotropy coordinate b_out is not the velocity anisotropy beta.

    Truth models use twice the fitting resolution in potential, velocity, moment, projection, action, and contour grids. A cold model at the fitting resolution must reproduce every mock bin within 0.1 adopted errors before fitting starts. This comparison includes the photometric profile after profiling its zero point.

    The mocks retain the saved 89 kinematic bins, stellar radial selection nodes, proper-motion units, asymmetric errors, and 82 photometric radii. Their values are replaced by noiseless equilibrium predictions. Streaming is zero, and every profile samples the same stellar mass/light DF. Photometric errors retain the earlier diagnostic weighting; neither these errors nor the mock objective define an adopted inference prior or a calibrated model-selection likelihood.

    Six fits¶

    Both mocks are fitted with a free cored halo from two distinct starting points. The no-DM mock also has two fits with the halo fixed to zero. Free-halo fits have nine coordinates: five stellar controls, remnant mass and radius, and halo density and radius. No-halo fits have seven. Halo density is bounded linearly between 0 and 10 solar masses per cubic parsec, so zero is permitted exactly. The halo radius is unidentified at zero density and is not a recovery criterion. The distance, halo inner slope, and outer taper remain fixed.

    The two starting points bracket the injected stellar mass and differ in every free coordinate. They are the same for the two mocks. The fitter never starts at the injected parameter vector. Bounded least squares uses coordinates scaled to [0,1], with a finite-difference step of 0.002 in these coordinates. The step remains finite at zero halo density. Each attempt is limited to 180 actual equilibrium evaluations, including derivative probes and rejected trials. A budget-limited fit is recorded as unconverged.

    Nearby evaluations can reuse the last converged stellar potential as an iteration seed. All scientific controls are rebuilt for each trial, and each selected solution receives both a cold replay and a fully refined cold solve. Identical coordinate evaluations within an attempt reuse their saved residuals.

    Acceptance criteria¶

    Each fit records these checks separately:

    • The optimizer terminates within its budget.
    • Cold replay and resolution refinement each shift residuals by less than 0.1 adopted errors; independent projected moments agree within 0.5%.
    • Refined mock residuals have RMS below 0.1 and maximum below 0.3 adopted errors.
    • Stellar mass is recovered within 5%, and total enclosed mass within 10% at every diagnostic radius from 0.5 to 80 pc.
    • Injected nonzero density at 20 pc is recovered within 10%. For the no-DM mock, recovered density must be below 0.1 solar masses per cubic parsec.

    These are predefined diagnostic thresholds. Agreement of different starts must also be inspected; no single best run certifies uniqueness. The saved profiles separate stars, remnants, halo, and total mass. A good total mass profile need not imply that all components or their scale radii are identified.

    Preservation and execution¶

    The driver freezes source, configurations, tests, and input snapshots. Hashes are checked before a batch starts or resumes. Model improvements and statuses are written atomically, and every evaluated trial is appended to a flushed log. Controller and worker locks prevent duplicate runs. After an interruption, rerunning the batch restarts unfinished fits from their saved best parameters; it does not restore optimizer memory. Completed fits, including capped attempts, are retained and skipped. Extending a capped fit requires an explicitly recorded follow-up experiment.

    PY=python
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py prepare --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py run --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME --detach
$PY bin/run_compact_df_recovery.py report --out results/df/CHOOSE_FRESH_NAME

    Two single-threaded workers run at most. PNG reports go to plots/, with their arrays and provenance under results/plot_data/. The result directory contains the immutable mocks, per-fit histories, checkpoints, validation, and summaries.

    This stage checks recovery and optimization within a matched stellar and dark density family. Independent lowered-isothermal mocks and fits, halo mismatch, noise, selection differences, mass-to-light gradients, and real-data inference follow separately. Passing this stage alone does not establish unbiased DM inference for Omega Cen.

    Launch record: 23 September 2026¶

    The active batch is results/df/compact_recovery_20260923_v2/, frozen from commit 01992f0. It launched at 00:02:56 UTC with two workers and six jobs, each limited to 180 trial equilibrium evaluations. Its controller.log, per-fit status.json, evaluations.jsonl, and best.json record progress. The launch is not a completed recovery result.

    Both regenerated truths passed the nominal-resolution check: maximum residual shifts were 0.00703 adopted errors for no DM and 0.00686 for the injected halo. The preceding two-evaluation smoke test passed numerical validation, with cold replay shift 0.0228 errors, refinement shift 0.0000662 errors, and maximum independent projection discrepancy 0.0326%. It was intentionally capped and failed the optimizer/physical-recovery gates, as required.

    The v1 smoke test exposed the documented polar-mapper failure. Its source, inputs, and failed outputs remain preserved; v1 was not launched as a fitting batch. The corrected implementation passed 64 focused tests and the complete previously failing refined-model rebuild before v2 launched.

    Progress figure: plots/compact_recovery_20260923_v2.png. Its arrays and provenance are in results/plot_data/compact_recovery_20260923_v2.json. Interim curves are labelled explicitly and are not mass measurements.

    v2 result: 23 September 2026¶

    All six fits finished by 01:34 UTC and all fail the combined gate; none terminated within 180 evaluations. Free-halo fits reproduce the noiseless mocks to 0.006-0.024 adopted errors RMS and total mass within 4.8%, but do not separate stars from DM. The no-DM mock yields rho20 = 1.42 and 2.34 with stellar mass 15% and 24% low. The cored mock yields rho20 = 2.53 and 3.08 (truth 2.0) with stellar mass 4% and 9% low. The fixed-no-halo fits recover stellar mass within 0.7%; start0 misses the maximum-residual gate (0.356). Cored start1 fails projection validation (pmr 0.81%). Per-fit numbers are in JOURNAL.md and report.json; the final figure is plots/compact_recovery_20260923_v2.png.

    Degeneracy figure: plots/compact_recovery_20260923_v2_degeneracy.png (script bin/plot_compact_degeneracy.py). The injected truth has objective 2.3e-4 at fitting resolution. The recovered solutions reach 6e-3 to 0.1, so they are unconverged. Those differences are still negligible against realistic chi-square scatter (about 18 for 171 points).
